  Trends Shaping LKN — June 2026  
📉

NC Job Growth Slowing — Talent Competition Intensifies

NC's year-over-year job growth cooled to 0.8% in early 2026. Slower growth means the competition for skilled talent intensifies locally. Workforce readiness and soft skills training are now the local competitive edge.

WORKFORCE
💸

Wages Losing the Race to Cost of Living

NC wages grew 3.4% but housing costs grew 3.8% and energy surged 6%. This affordability gap directly impacts who can afford to live and work in LKN — and shapes the workforce available to local employers.

ECONOMICS
🏥

Duke Health's $284M Acquisition Anchors LKN Identity

Duke Health's 2025 acquisition of Lake Norman Regional Medical Center signals LKN's growing status as a regional healthcare hub — more jobs, more credential demand, and more opportunity for community health partnerships.

HEALTHCARE · ECONOMY
🤝

Nonprofit Collaboration Is the 2026 Survival Strategy

73% of nonprofits that collaborate achieve expanded services, increased funding, and better outcomes. In 2026, going it alone is a risk most organizations simply cannot afford.

NONPROFITS
🤖

AI Governance Is the #1 Compliance Issue for Every Sector

From HR hiring tools to legal documentation to educational platforms — AI governance frameworks must be built NOW, before exposure. June's Visionary Circle speaker Nils Tracy addresses exactly this.
